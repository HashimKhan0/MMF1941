## Item 7 — unresolved data limitations

Ten items. None is fatal; all are documented rather than worked around.

### Affecting the estimand

**1 · Iron ore is average-settled.** `SCO1` settles on the arithmetic average of
the month's IODEX prints, so the futures return is a shrinking fraction of the
real move as the month progresses — pass-through (N−k+1)/N. This **inflates** β
by roughly 50% and, because the other three pairs are undistorted, breaks the
cross-pair comparison that is the project's purpose. Handled by DD-12: baseline
restricted to the first 5 trading days of each contract month, with the full
sample rescaled as robustness and a day-of-month bucket test run before the
freeze.

**2 · No spot iron ore index obtainable.** TSI and Platts assessments refused on
entitlement (`LHSI0006`, `PFAMCFRC`); `ISIX62IU` (Custeel) untested when the API
was suspended; WRDS carries only the Datastream Commodities *sample*, with the
full product unsubscribed. The spot index is the only unattenuated instrument
and it is not available.

**3 · Settlement times unverified.** `DES` unavailable. Expected gaps against a
17:00 ET FX snap are ~2.5h (WTI, Brent), ~3h (LME copper) and **~10.5h (SGX iron
ore)**. If the iron ore figure holds, the same-day AUD FX observation contains
most of a trading day *after* the commodity print — inflating the contemporaneous
β and deflating the predictive β, both toward the expected result, on the pair
already carrying limitation 1. Sourcing from exchange specifications is Phase 0
work and does not need Bloomberg. DD-18.

**4 · The dollar control is the Fed H.10 broad index, not ICE DXY.** Identified

## Three findings from the Phase 3 diagnostics

**1 · AUD fails the date-shift test on substance (DD-19).** Its −1 shift
coefficient (0.0227, t = 2.51) is *larger* than lag 0 (0.0166). That is the
signature of misalignment, and it compounds a 10.5-hour commodity-to-FX timing
gap (DD-18) and average-price settlement (DD-12). Three independent problems on
one pair.

**2 · The DD-19 significance criterion is uninformative at this sample size.**
At n ≈ 3,700, a coefficient a ninth the size of the contemporaneous effect still
clears |t| = 1.96, so the criterion tests power rather than alignment. The rule
was declared before the test ran and **has not been changed**; both options are
set out in DD-19 for the supervisor to decide.

**3 · The DD-12 bucket test is inconclusive, not confirmatory.** Raw AUD β rises
from 0.0145 (days 1–4) to 0.0377 (days 17–25), directionally as 1/w predicts but
only 2.6× against the theoretical 5.7×, and every bucket is insignificant. So the
8-day baseline is **not** vindicated by this test — it is merely not refuted.
Worth saying plainly rather than claiming the mechanism is established.

Also note the AUD contemporaneous β of ~0.017 sits far below the 0.10–0.25
sanity band. CLP (0.18) is inside it, NOK (0.098) just below, CAD (0.061) below.
Per the project's own rule, that gets said rather than reported quietly.
